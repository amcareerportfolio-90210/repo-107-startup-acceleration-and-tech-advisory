### Technology & Strategy Advisory (Qwykr Technologies)

Welcome to the technology and business advisory portfolio of **Anand Manikutty**. This repository documents strategic consulting, startup acceleration initiatives, and media technology platforms developed during my tenure as Technology & Business Consultant and Senior Director at Qwykr Technologies from January 2018 to September 2023.

---

### Core Advisory & Leadership Highlights
- **Multi-Sector Leadership:** Spearheaded strategic and technical initiatives across farming, product design, and software systems—driving measurable operational impact across tech and digital media sectors.
- **Media Strategy & Co-Founding:** Co-founded Pravasi Indians magazine (originally launched as Namaste Bharat), shaping platform architecture, brand strategy, and market positioning. Designed and built digital delivery platforms tailored for the publishing and media industries.
- **Top-Tier Accelerator Incubations:** Played a pivotal role in securing placements for Qwykr Technologies (in partnership with The Indian Quest Digital) in premier startup programs:
  - Microsoft for Startups
  - Google for Startups
  - Wadhwani Foundation's IGNITE Program
  - IIM Bangalore NSR CEL's WSP Program
  - Xartup for Startups
- **Impact-Driven Frameworks (RALPH Platform):** Led the architectural development of low-cost, open-access technology frameworks—including the RALPH platform—prioritizing social utility, academic collaboration, and transparent tech over purely commercial ends. Engaged directly with international researchers, including two Nobel Laureates, to review and validate these platforms.
